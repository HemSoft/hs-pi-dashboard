CI had never passed — every run since the v0.1 push died in ~5s at GitHub's
action-resolution phase: the pinned `actions/setup-go` SHA ended `…303a` but
upstream v7.0.0 is `…303e`. Corrected the final hex digit and added
`scripts/check-action-pins.sh` + a `pin-check` workflow as a regression guard
(it verifies every `uses:` pin against the real upstream tag SHA from outside
the resolution phase, which no step inside ci.yml can do). Confirmed
diagnosis: transcription typo, not a moved tag or renamed repo.
